Bitcoin Book of BIPs

compiled by Adam Shannon

Deployed and complete Bitcoin Improvement Proposals, grouped by theme and bound as a book. The BIP text is unchanged from the community repository; the reading order and chapter introductions are new.

Start with process and addresses if you are new. Consensus, Taproot, wallets, and the peer-to-peer network follow.
